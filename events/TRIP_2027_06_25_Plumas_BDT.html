About the Area

Plumas National Forest covers over a million acres in the Northern Sierra Nevada between Quincy, Graeagle, and Lake Almanor. By late June, high-country forest roads, mountain lakes, and Backcountry Discovery Trail (BDT) connectors open up through towering pine forests and granite ridges.

Fire Restrictions & Permits

Current as of November 18, 2026

Fire Restrictions: Plumas National Forest (USFS) enforces seasonal Stage I/II fire restrictions during summer. Always check active forest orders before departure. A free California Campfire Permit is required for camp stoves, lanterns, and any permitted campfires outside developed campgrounds.

Get Ready Before We Go

🛑 SAFETY REQUIREMENTS

Rear Amber Chase Light and HAM Radio communications are REQUIRED to travel with the group.

Why Amber Chase Lights?

Dusty trails are common – amber lights penetrate dust far better than white or red, helping prevent rear-end collisions in low visibility. Learn more about Why Chase Lights?

HAM Radio

Program to 146.460 MHz (simplex). Handhelds work great even without a license for listening.

If you don't have a ham radio yet, check out our guide to get started.
