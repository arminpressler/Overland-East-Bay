About the Area

Anza-Borrego Desert State Park is California's largest state park, spanning over 600,000 acres of the Colorado Desert east of San Diego. Late February brings mild desert temperatures, rugged badlands overlooks like Fonts Point, narrow slot canyons, sandy desert washes, and extensive backcountry dirt roads.

Fire Restrictions & Permits

Current as of November 18, 2026

Fire Restrictions: Anza-Borrego Desert State Park prohibits ground fires park-wide; campfires are only allowed in raised metal fire containers or provided campground rings (bring your own firewood — no wood gathering). On surrounding BLM lands, a free California Campfire Permit is required for portable gas/propane stoves.

Get Ready Before We Go

🛑 SAFETY REQUIREMENTS

Rear Amber Chase Light and HAM Radio communications are REQUIRED to travel with the group.

Why Amber Chase Lights?

Dusty trails are common – amber lights penetrate dust far better than white or red, helping prevent rear-end collisions in low visibility. Learn more about Why Chase Lights?

HAM Radio

Program to 146.460 MHz (simplex). Handhelds work great even without a license for listening.

If you don't have a ham radio yet, check out our guide to get started.
