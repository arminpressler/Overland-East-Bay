Let's start getting ready for 2027

What we did this year, what's already on the calendar for 2027, and two places I need your help.

2026 — We Made Memories

Last year around this time I set out to improve our communications channels beyond our meetups. The Google email group was the first step, and then it was followed by the www.overland-eastbay.com site. So I figured it's a good time to check in and see how this is working for everybody.

We met nine-ish times under the oak tree (and skipped a couple on purpose, because trips and life happen). Along the way Roger showed us how not to winch ourselves into a tree, Scott explained why my gear organization is wrong, Mike N got fires started with things I would have walked past, and Derek's new camper made the rest of us rethink our setups. We also finally put the Group Trip Agreement and the glovebox checklist on the site, which were long overdue.

On the trail it was a good year: Mojave Trail Guardian in the spring and again in October, the Owyhee run this summer, Frazer Flats in September, Mike Norton's Hwy 89/80 BDR weekend at the end of September, and Death Valley Basecamp just wrapped a few days ago.

We definitely made memories this year — thanks to everyone who led, swept, tried something new on the trail, cooked, or quietly handed out a strap, stumbled through some poison ivy.

2027 — Let's Plan for Even More

The 2027 calendar has a very early first version up on the site now — none of it is settled — this will change, but I figured it's a starting point for our Dec 6th planning session. Each one has a card on the site, and the calendar page carries the full list along with the monthly meetups. All of them are marked "planning," so let's go shape this; we can make it more interesting.

What's still open — and where I need your help

Nothing after September. October, November and December 2027 are empty. If you've been sitting on a fall trip, that's the slot to claim.

Every trip needs a lead: someone who owns the route, the camping, and the go/no-go call.

And the smaller pieces: who wants to research a route, who wants to sweep, who knows a camp in any of these places. Ten small "I'll take that one" replies beats one heroic volunteer.

We'll build the calendar out at the December meetup — Sunday, December 6, 1–3 PM at The Growler in Danville (back room, indoor, warm, and yes there is beer). Bring your wish list and a date you'd defend. (Regular Pleasanton Ridge meetups pick back up Sunday, January 10.)

A V1 Draft

  • Feb 18–21 — Anza-Borrego: Winter desert, and honestly the friendliest desert driving we've got.
  • Mar 11–14 — Death Valley, meeting the Idaho crew: Spring desert meetup in the park with the Idaho crew.
  • Apr 30 – May 9 — Utah Pony Express: The long one. Start working on your kitchen passes now.
  • May 28–30 — Explore the Old Highway 1: Old coast alignments and Los Padres backcountry.
  • Jun 25–27 — Plumas BDT: Backcountry run through Plumas National Forest.
  • Aug 13–15 — Levitt Lake, Hwy 108: High alpine, small high-clearance rigs, near Sonora Pass.
  • Sep 15–19 — White Pocket / Grand Canyon: Multi-day Southwest overland trip, White Pocket and the Grand Canyon backcountry.

GoogleGroup and www.overland-eastbay.com

Back to our communications channels beyond our meetups. The Google email group was the first step, and then it was followed by the www.overland-eastbay.com site. You may have noticed we have a couple of new features and capabilities:

  • A search function — you can search the whole site now, guides and trips included.
  • A more flexible calendar page, with more options for navigating it.
  • Our resource pages are steadily growing — guides, checklists, training notes, all in one place.
  • And I'd rather hear what's wrong with it from you than guess. So: what's broken, what's confusing, or what's missing?

So I figured it's a good time to check in and see how this is working for everybody.

  • Is the calendar accurate and easy to read?
  • Do the trip pages actually tell you what you need to know before you commit?
  • Can you find the guides and checklists when you want them, on your phone, in the field?
  • Is there anything you keep looking for that simply isn't there? (And is search finding it?)

Just reply to this email (or send me a text) with whatever comes to mind — one line is plenty. Or grab me at the December meetup. I'd rather have ten small "this bugs me" notes than one more person quietly giving up on the site.
